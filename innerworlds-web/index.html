Where things are kept

Reflections, notes, feeling words, colour picks, saved paintings, preferences and your own paintings are stored in this browser, on this device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Works shown from the Metropolitan Museum of Art come from its Open Access collection and load from the museum’s servers, so the museum can see that this browser asked for them. Their colours are measured on this device to sort them. Storage here is not encrypted and not protected by a password, so anyone using this browser profile can reach it, and clearing browser data removes it. Copy a backup from My World if you want a copy.

What is generated, and what is not

Every question, essay, drift sequence and walk is written ahead of time and is the same for everyone. Suggestions and patterns in the Journal are counted on this device from your own entries. The texts in this first edition were drafted with AI assistance.

The painting is the one thing made for you. It is drawn on your device from your palette and mark style, and nothing leaves this machine. Your reflection is never used to make it.

AI reflections

These are off unless you turn them on in the Journal. When you press “Ask for a reflection”, your twelve most recent entries and notes (the question, what you wrote, your feeling words and colour) are sent to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, which writes a short reflection back. Nothing is sent at any other time. The reflection can be wrong and is not medical or psychological advice.

Sharing

Sharing copies the title and the day's question. It never includes your reflection, your feeling words or your colour pick. A backup does include your reflections in plain text.

What this is not

Reflections are creative prompts, not assessment, therapy or prediction. The Sky tab shows where the Moon and Sun are today, which is the same for everyone. The lens itself is off unless you turn it on. If you do, your birth details stay on this device, the chart is calculated here, and it only adds a colour and a line to your day. Nothing in the app predicts anything. There are no streaks, no notifications, no analytics, no advertising and no background generation. Quiet days are shown as quiet days.

Not in this version

Accounts, cloud storage, cross-device sync, shared chapters, printed books and subscriptions.

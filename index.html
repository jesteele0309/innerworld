Where things are kept

Reflections, feeling words, preferences and paintings are stored in this browser, on this device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Storage here is not encrypted and not protected by a password, so anyone using this browser profile can reach it, and clearing browser data removes it. Export a backup from My World if you want a copy.

What is made by a person, and what is not

Every question, essay, drift sequence and walk in INNERWORLD is written by a person. Nothing you read or hear here is generated.

The painting is the one generated thing. By default it is drawn on your device from your palette and mark style, and nothing leaves this machine. If a painting model is configured, it receives the question, the palette and the mark style — and nothing else. Your reflection is never part of that request, and the server refuses any request carrying extra fields.

Sharing

Sharing sends the title and the day's question. It never includes your reflection or your feeling words. Printing a chapter includes titles and questions, not reflections. A backup export does include your reflections in plain text.

What this is not

Reflections are creative prompts, not assessment, therapy or prediction. There is no birth chart, no calculated astrology and no prediction anywhere in the app. There are no streaks, no notifications, no analytics, no advertising and no background generation. Quiet days are shown as quiet days.

Not yet built

Accounts, cloud storage, cross-device sync, backup restore, shared chapters, printed books and subscriptions are not implemented in this version. The chapter print button uses your browser's own print dialog.
